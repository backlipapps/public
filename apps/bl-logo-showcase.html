A scrolling logo carousel for the storefront trust strip — client logos, partner brands, press mentions, or trust badges. Native versions for Shopify and Wix.

On Shopify: layout, speed, and style controls, responsive presentation, direction left/right or none, pause-on-hover, and a standalone full-width option — up to seven logo images per section in the audited build. On Wix: continuous scrolling with adjustable speed, direction, logo size, and spacing.

A visual presentation tool. It does not verify logo ownership, approve usage rights, or create endorsements.
