Catalog › BL Bulk Product Image Uploader

The native-marketplace alternative to spreadsheet-and-CSV image imports, for merchants who add products in batches. Native versions for Shopify, Wix, WooCommerce, and Shopware.

Matches files to products by SKU, product ID, or handle — name files like TSHIRT-RED.1.jpg, review every match, then add images or carefully overwrite. Supported intake in the audited builds: JPEG, PNG, GIF, and WebP.

Treat overwrite as a controlled operation and test it on a small batch first. Platform-specific matching, overwrite, and gallery behavior varies — each listing shows what that build supports.
